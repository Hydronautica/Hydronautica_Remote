What is drawn. The exact Peregrine solution of the flume form of the nonlinear Schrödinger equation, with the second-order bound harmonic added, so crests are sharper than troughs. The wave train switches on at the paddle and its front travels down the flume at the group velocity. The far end absorbs, so nothing reflects.

What is not drawn. Higher-order effects shift the real focus by a few tenths of a metre and make the group lopsided. The model also does not capture breaking itself. Expect to fine-tune the focus distance in the lab. All runs here are in still water, with the current off.

Reading steepness. kη is the carrier wavenumber times the crest height. The theoretical limit for a steady wave is about 0.44. Focusing groups usually start to break at lower values, around 0.3 to 0.4, often as spilling whitecaps at the crest.
